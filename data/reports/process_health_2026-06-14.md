## TOP 3 PROCESS IMPROVEMENTS

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. PHYSICS-FIRST DESIGN GATE
   Pattern: 4 of 9 design issues were proxy approximations
            (speed table, distance threshold, fixed-gain, scalar collapse).
   Action:  Before any multi-variable threshold or lookup table is added,
            require a dimensional-analysis doc comment: "Physical quantity
            being approximated: X. Why not compute directly: Y."
            Reject PRs where Y is "not tried" or "unknown".
   Impact:  Eliminates the dominant sub-category at design review time,
            before any E2E cycles are spent.

2. E2E VALIDATION GATE FOR TUNING CHANGES
   Pattern: 4 prevention themes called for regression/CI gates. The worst
            single recovery (82.5 pts across 4 tracks) came from a
            bundled undocumented tuning change that skipped E2E.
   Action:  Enforce: any commit touching av_stack_config.yaml or
            _MPC_WEIGHT_AUTO_DERIVE_PARAMS must have a comment block:
            "E2E validated: <date>, tracks: <list>, score delta: <±n>".
            Add a pre-commit grep that fails if the block is missing.
   Impact:  Prevents the highest-deduction class of regression (hidden
            tuning drift that only shows up at highway curves).

3. EXPLICIT PHASE-GATE CHECKLIST FOR STEERING TERMS
   Pattern: 3 issues (phase-gated FF, proportional curve anticipation,
            term_time leak) all shared the same root: a term was active
            in driving phases where it should be zero.
   Action:  Add a code-review checklist item: for every new steering-
            contribution term, explicitly document which phase(s) it is
            active in and assert/gate on those phases. Any "always active"
            term requires justification.
   Impact:  Catches the "continuous FF on straight segments" class of bug
            at review time rather than after 2–3 E2E runs.
```
