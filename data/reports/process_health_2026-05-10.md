   Pattern:  Signal routing errors (entries 1, 11) and transition

   Action:   Write lightweight correlation/phase-gating contract

               < jerk threshold
   Impact:   Catches signal routing and transition bugs at

   Pattern:  Entry 13 — undocumented q_lat 2× doubling bundled

   Impact:   Prevents the class of "bundled tuning regression"
