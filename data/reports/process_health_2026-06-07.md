   Pattern: 2 of 3 implementation issues were signal routing failures — a
            term computed for one driving phase (ENTRY, curve approach) leaking

   Impact:  Would have prevented the term_time leak and the FF-on-straights
            jerk oscillation without requiring a diagnostic cycle. Pairs with
            the existing "phase-gate all feedforward terms by default" design
